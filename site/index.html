Printable checklists, four at a time

Open a page, hit Ctrl/Cmd+P, check things off with an actual pen. Each page prints as its own little packet.

1 · The Budget Worksheet

Monthly budget with the real rent number, the move-in bill math, and the "do you actually have the cash" check. Go with Chapter 1.

2 · Walkthrough Checklists

The apartment-tour list and the full move-in inspection form — the one that protects your deposit. Go with Chapter 4.

3 · Move-Out & Deposit Packet

The two-week countdown, the deep-clean blitz, the move-out walkthrough, and the deposit timeline. Go with Chapter 7.

4 · First-Week Setup

Utilities, the new-place shopping kit, and everything to do in the first seven days. Go with Chapter 8.

This is education, not legal advice. Rules about deposits, notice periods, and repairs vary by state and city. When a checklist says "check your local rules," that's your cue to look at your state's official tenant resources or a local legal-aid organization.
