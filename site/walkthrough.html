A. The Apartment Tour (you get ~15 minutes; this makes them count)

Kitchen

  • Faucet: hot water arrives, drains fast
  • Fridge & oven: open, look inside, check smells
  • Burners: click each one; ask gas or electric
  • Under-sink: leaks, dampness, mildew smell
  • Dishwasher: runs / ask when last serviced

Bathroom

  • Toilet: two flushes, refills and stops on its own
  • Shower: hot, good pressure, doesn't die when sink runs
  • Tub caulk & grout: black spots = mold conversation
  • Exhaust fan actually pulls air

Every room

  • Windows open, close, and LOCK
  • Light switches + outlets tested (bring a charger)
  • Ceiling stains or bows; soft spots on floor
  • Doors open/close/lock; count the keys promised
  • Cell signal in the farthest room

Building & block

  • Hallways clean, lights working
  • Laundry on-site (cost per load)
  • Parking situation known
  • Walk the block at your usual arrival time

Ask while keys are in hand

  • Average utility bill here?
  • Repair contact + how requests are sent?
  • Heat type (radiator / gas furnace / electric)?
  • Anything changed between tenants?
  • Planned construction or fee changes?

B. Move-In Inspection (the MONEY walkthrough — do before your stuff comes in)

Unit: ______________ Move-in date: ____________ Date of form: ____________
One room at a time. Photo every defect (wide shot + close-up). Be specific about locations. No defect is too small — walls are where deposits go to die.

Kitchen

  • Countertops: burns, chips, stains — photographed
  • Cabinets (inside, outside, hinges, hardware)
  • Appliances present + running (fridge line, oven light, burners)
  • Sink, faucet, sprayer, disposal + dry under-sink
  • Floors: cracked tile / lifting vinyl / carpet seams
  • Walls & ceiling: scuffs, holes, grease marks

Bathroom

  • Toilet seat, lid, covers, no constant running
  • Tub: caulk, grout, drain speed, chips, rust
  • Sink drains fast; faucet solid; cabinet inside empty/clean
  • Mirror / medicine cabinet doors
  • Exhaust fan pulls (tissue test)
  • Walls & ceiling: stains near the shower

Bedrooms & living areas

  • Walls/ceiling: holes, stains, odors (smell each room)
  • Closets: doors, rods, shelf
  • Windows: open/close/lock, screens intact, blinds complete
  • Doors: hardware, locks, keys counted
  • Smoke + CO detectors present and tested
  • Outlets/switches/lights all work

Entry, systems, misc

  • Deadbolt, peephole, chain; every key/fob/mailbox key counted: ____________
  • Thermostat works; ask who controls heat
  • Water heater: no leak at base
  • HVAC filter present (know who supplies the next one)
  • Trash/recycling day noted
  • Parking spot # noted: ____________
  • Meter readings photographed (if visible)

Close the loop (same day!)

  • Email photos + this form to landlord: subject "Move-in condition photos — [address] — [date]"
  • Kept my own backup copy (cloud, not just the phone)
  • Got (or requested) acknowledgment / signed form copy
