3 · Move-Out & Deposit Packet

Companion to Chapter 7 (docs/07-deposit-recovery.md). Check your state's deposit-return deadline first; the rest of this packet is scheduled around it.

Deadline check: most states require the deposit + itemized deductions back within a set window (commonly 14–45 days after move-out). Look up your state's official tenant page — don't rely on a general number.

A. The Countdown

  • 30–60 days out: written notice given exactly as the lease requires (copy kept); deposit clause re-read
  • 2–3 weeks out: deep clean booked/started; joint move-out walkthrough requested (a specific date, in writing)
  • 1 week out: walls patched if lease requires; photos taken; boxes mostly packed
  • Move-out day: walkthrough with landlord if possible; keys + fobs returned with count noted; forwarding address given in writing
  • At the state deadline: deposit + itemized statement due — if not, send the inquiry letter (section D)

B. The Deep Clean (deductions often claimed: cleaning, walls, carpet, missing items)

Kitchen

  • Oven + stovetop: grate-level clean (including knob halo)
  • Fridge inside/out AND behind + underneath
  • Microwave; counters; cabinet fronts (grease is high)
  • Sink shined; range hood filter; dishwasher filter
  • Floors mopped

Bathrooms

  • Toilet: base, brackets, behind
  • Tub/shower: grout scrubbed, drain clear and visible
  • Mirror; vanity inside/out; exhaust-fan cover
  • Floors

Every room

  • Walls: marks wiped, holes filled if lease asks
  • Baseboards; window sills and tracks; blinds
  • Light fixtures, switch plates
  • Closets empty + wiped

Floors & the scrutinized spots

  • Vacuum the edges (they betray skipped vacuuming), spot-treat carpet
  • Professional carpet cleaning w/ receipt if lease requires — receipt kept
  • Hard floors mopped
  • Behind/under fridge + washer-dryer hookups
  • Balcony/patio; entry closet; on top of the fridge; door tops/frames
  • "After" photos: whole-room wide shots + close-ups of former problem spots

C. Move-Out Walkthrough Companion

  • Move-in photos + signed condition form in hand
  • Repair log handy
  • Room-by-room vs move-in form; every landlord note written down verbatim with my response (e.g., "present at move-in — photo 14")
  • Form signed by both — I photographed each signed page
  • All keys/fobs/remotes returned; count noted: ____________
  • Forwarding address provided in writing, dated
  • No joint walkthrough offered? Self-conducted one done anyway + photos emailed same day with forwarding address

D. Deposit Inquiry Letter (copy, adapt, send — keep copies)

Subject: Security deposit — [address, unit] — move-out date [date]

Hi [name],

I vacated [address] on [date] and provided a forwarding address the same day. As of [state deadline date], I haven't received the deposit or an itemized statement of deductions. Could you send both this week? My records (move-in and move-out photos, signed condition form, repair log) are attached for reference. Thanks, [name, new address, phone]

  • Sent first inquiry (dated, copies kept)
  • Follow-up in 7–10 days, second channel added (call log: who, when)
  • Written demand dated with a resolve-by date, remedies named
  • Exhibit list assembled in one folder, files numbered 01–09
  • Local help looked up (tenant hotline / legal aid / small claims is a do-it-yourself court in most places)
