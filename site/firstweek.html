A. Utilities — schedule these, in this order

Internet first (installation appointments book 1–2 weeks out), then electricity/gas (1–3 business days), then confirm who handles water/trash.

  • Internet: appointment booked before move-in day
  • Electricity: active by 9am move-in morning, account name matches lease
  • Gas: activated; ask about average bill while you're in there
  • Water/sewer/trash: confirmed who pays and the pickup day
  • Renter's insurance: effective on lease day 1, landlord added as interested party
  • Old address utilities: still on until old walkthrough is done

B. The New-Place Kit (buy once, stop thinking about it)

  • Shower curtain + liner + rings; bath mat; 2 towels per person
  • Toilet paper, hand soap, dish soap, sponges
  • Trash bags (checked against the actual bin size)
  • All-purpose cleaner + paper towels
  • TOILET PLUNGER (the eternal first-apartment lesson)
  • One pot, one pan, plates/bowls/cups for 4, utensils, one good knife, cutting board, coffee or tea situation
  • Salt, pepper, cooking oil, jar opener
  • Light bulbs (walk each fixture first — ceilings love weird sizes) + one power strip
  • Curtains/rods if bedrooms are bare (sleep is infrastructure)
  • Door mat in; bed off the floor
  • Small kitchen-rated fire extinguisher (~$25 of quiet confidence)

C. The Seven Days

  • Walkthrough done BEFORE anything came in (photos emailed same day)
  • Breaker panel labeled — flip and verify the map once, at noon not midnight
  • Address updated: bank, employer/payroll, phone, insurance, ID as required, subscriptions, voter registration
  • Asked in writing whether locks were re-keyed between tenants (do not DIY)
  • Met the people sharing your wall/ceiling/floor — 30 seconds each
  • Found: mail room, trash chute, recycling, storage, laundry app/coins
  • Smoke + CO detectors tested again
  • Bed made first; "first open" boxes unpacked before the judging hallway boxes
  • Something that smells/looks like home installed (candle, plant, soft lamp)

D. Move-out prep began today (yes, really)

  • Repair log started (one note; one running table)
  • Photo folder named for this apartment, dates in filenames
  • Monthly 10-minute photo walk in the calendar (under-sinks, sills, water heater)
